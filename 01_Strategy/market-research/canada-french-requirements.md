# Canadian French language requirements (checked 2026-10-08)

Secondary sources; re-confirm on canada.ca (IRCC) before publishing anything to learners.

| Goal | Level needed | Skills tested | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citizenship (ages 18-54) | NCLC 4 (about A2) | Speaking + listening only | TEF Canada, TCF Canada (or proof from education) |
| Express Entry French bonus | NCLC 7 (about B1/B2) in all 4 skills | Reading, writing, listening, speaking | TEF Canada, TCF Canada |

- Express Entry: NCLC 7 in French + CLB 5 English = up to 50 extra CRS points; strong French with weak English = 25.
- French-language category draws in 2026 had cut-offs around 382-420 CRS (far below general draws) - strong pull.
- Federal target for francophone PR admissions outside Quebec: 9% (2026) -> 9.5% (2027) -> 10.5% (2028).
- Test results usually valid 2 years.

Implication: the founder's "B1/B2" note maps to immigration (Express Entry), while citizenship is a lower,
speaking+listening-only bar. These are two different tracks with different roadmaps.
