Security gaps
Learners can edit their own profiles, placement results, check-ins, estimates, plans, tasks, and reports. These records are private from other learners, but their values are not tamper-proof.
Correct answer IDs are available to signed-in learners through the question bank.
Task ownership for problem reports is checked in the app, not enforced by the data rules. A direct record write could attach a forged task reference or workflow status to a report.
The data store does not guarantee unique records or atomic updates, so simultaneous submissions could create duplicate plans or check-ins.
Practice generation and writing scoring have no rate limit. AI-generated practice content is not human-reviewed by design.
