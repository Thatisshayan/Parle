### Security gaps

- Learners can change or delete their own results, including scores and timestamps. The 14-day limit is an app-level check, not tamper-proof enforcement.
- Signed-in users can read question records that include answer keys. The browser handles auto-scoring, so test answers and results are not trustworthy evidence.
- Simultaneous retries can create duplicate records; the platform does not provide an atomic uniqueness guarantee.
- The writing service checks for a signed-in Parle learner and saved learner profile, but has no per-learner usage limit.
