Buildy GuideFrom idea to launchHomeGetting StartedHow Buildy WorksFeaturesDashboardHelp CenterTech SupportDashboard → Website SettingsWebsite Settings is where you control how your website presents itself: its name, icon, Buildy branding badge, and a one-click code export. Open it from the Dashboard under Your website when you want to rename the site, update the logo, or download a copy of the source.The Website Settings screenClick to expandThe Website Settings section What is inside Site BrandingSet your website name and website icon. The name and icon show in browser tabs, search results, project cards, and as your site's logo.For the icon, upload your own file or let Buildy generate one with AI. Keep the name short and on-brand so it reads cleanly in a tab. Buildy BrandingToggle Show Branding Badge to control the small Made with Buildy badge on your site. Whether you can hide it depends on your plan.Buildy affiliates can add an optional Affiliate ID. When visitors sign up through the badge, you can earn commission on those referrals. Code ExportDownload your website's source code as a zip. Use this for a backup or to share the project with a developer. Everyday building and publishing do not require an export. How it works with chatYou can change many of these settings by asking Buildy directly. Chat is often faster than hunting through menus when you already know what you want.Try asking:Rename my website to Acme StudioGenerate a new website icon for my brandHow do I remove the Buildy badge?Export my website code as a zip Good to knowPublishing is elsewhereWebsite Settings does not control going live. Use the Publish & Share button at the top right when you are ready for the world to see your latest version.Access lives on Sign-in PageWho can see your website (public vs. login-only) is managed under Sign-in Page, not here. Visit that section to set visibility and access requirements.Name it like your brandPick a clear website name early. It shows in the browser tab, search results, and other places visitors notice, so a short brand name reads cleaner than a long internal project title.Pro tip: brand before you share the linkSet your real website name and icon (and check the badge setting) before you send the link to customers.
