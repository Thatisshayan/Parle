# Founding Tutor Recruitment Plan (decision #21)

Goal: **20 approved, Stripe-connected tutors live on launch day**, covering all three tracks and a wide spread of
time zones and explanation languages. Start before module 5 is built; first applicants test the vetting flow.

## Target mix (20 tutors)
| Need | Count |
|---|---|
| TEF/TCF Canada prep specialists | 8 |
| Citizenship / everyday conversation | 5 |
| General conversation and grammar | 7 |
| Time zones | At least 6 in the Americas, 6 in Europe/Africa, 3 in Asia/Middle East |
| Explanation languages beyond English/French | Persian, Arabic, Spanish, Hindi/Punjabi, Portuguese - at least 1 each |

## Founding-tutor offer (to confirm with founder)
- Commission **10% for the first 6 months** (instead of 20%), then the standard rate.
- "Founding tutor" badge permanently on the profile.
- Featured placement in search ties for the first 3 months.
- Paid work before launch: reviewing the placement question bank ($ per batch of 50 items) and calibration sessions
  with beta learners ($ per assessment). Gives early income and makes them invested.
- Direct line to the team for feedback.

## Funnel
| Stage | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Sourced | 120 | Lists from LinkedIn, French teacher Facebook/Telegram groups, alliance française alumni, TEF/TCF prep course instructors, founder network, referrals from interviewed tutors |
| Contacted | 100 | Personal message (template below), no mass email |
| Intro call | 40 | 15-minute call: explain Parle, offer, answer questions |
| Applied | 30 | Complete the in-app application (module 5) |
| Approved + Stripe connected | 20 | Admin review within 48h |

## Outreach message (template)
> Bonjour <name>, I'm building Parle, a French learning platform for people with a real deadline, mostly newcomers
> preparing for TEF/TCF Canada and citizenship. Every learner arrives with a tested level and a personal roadmap, and
> you get a short brief on their weak points before each lesson. We're inviting 20 founding tutors: 10% commission for
> the first 6 months and paid work reviewing our placement test before launch. Would you be open to a 15-minute call
> this week?

## Timeline
| When | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Module 1-4 build | Source 120, contact 100, run intro calls; recruit 2 question-bank reviewers immediately |
| Module 5 ready | First 10 applications go through the real vetting flow (also our test of it) |
| Module 6 ready | Stripe Connect onboarding with real tutors (test mode, then live at launch) |
| Pre-launch | 20 approved; calibration sessions with beta learners; availability filled for launch week |

## Rules
- Never contact tutors through another platform's messaging in a way that breaks its terms.
- Tutors in countries Stripe can't pay go on the waitlist (spec open question 10); don't promise dates.
- Track everything in `05_Operations/tutors/recruiting/pipeline.csv` (name, source, stage, specialty, time zone,
  languages, notes). No ID documents or personal data beyond contact details in the workstation.
