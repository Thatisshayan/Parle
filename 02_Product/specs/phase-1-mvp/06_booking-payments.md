# Module 6 - Booking, Lessons and Payments

Status: DRAFT · Phase 1 · Build order 6 of 8

## Purpose
Let a learner book and pay for a tutor lesson that is tied to their roadmap, and let the tutor get paid
80% after the lesson.

## 1. Tutor availability
- Weekly recurring availability in the tutor's time zone (e.g. Tue 18:00-21:00), plus date exceptions (time off,
  extra slots).
- Lesson lengths offered: 30, 45, 60 minutes (tutor can turn lengths off).
- Buffer between lessons: 0 / 10 / 15 minutes (tutor setting).
- Minimum notice for new bookings: 12 hours (tutor can raise it to 24 or 48).
- Booking window: up to 30 days ahead.

## 2. Booking flow (learner)
1. From a tutor profile or a "Suggested tutor lesson" in Today's Plan, choose length and slot (shown in learner's time zone).
2. Optional message to the tutor ("I have my TEF in 6 weeks").
3. Pay with Stripe Checkout (email must be verified).
4. Confirmed: both receive an email with a calendar file (.ics) and the meeting link; the lesson appears in
   Lessons (learner) and Dashboard (tutor) and on the learner's Today's Plan on that day.
5. Reminders by email 24 hours and 1 hour before.

## 3. Lesson brief (sent to tutor 24h before, also visible anytime before the lesson)
Student first name, goal and track, target and date, current level per skill, this week's focus, top 3 weaknesses
from the mistake log, last check-in result, notes from previous lessons with this tutor, learner's message, and
3 suggested activities generated by AI from that data. Labelled "Suggestions - you're the teacher."

## 4. After the lesson
- Tutor marks the lesson Completed (or Learner no-show) within 48 hours; a reminder is sent at 24 hours.
- Lesson notes form: skills worked on, each skill marked Struggling / OK / Strong, free notes, homework (optional).
- Notes feed the roadmap (Module 3, section 6) and appear in the learner's lesson history.
- If the tutor doesn't mark the lesson within 48 hours, it is auto-completed (no notes) unless the learner reported a problem.
- Learner gets a prompt to review (Module 5, section 5).

## 5. Money flow (Stripe Connect)
- Tutors onboard with Stripe Connect (Express accounts, using Stripe cross-border payouts where needed). Coverage is
  wide but not complete (e.g. Cameroon was not on Stripe's list when checked on 2026-10-08). The application form
  checks the tutor's country against the current list; tutors in unsupported countries can apply to a waitlist.
  Re-verify the list during the Buildy capability check.
- Minimum charge per lesson: $8 (30-minute lessons are only offered when the pro-rated price reaches $8), so card
  fees never swallow the commission.
- At checkout the learner pays the listed price in USD (Stripe shows local currency where available).
- Parle holds the payment. The tutor's 80% is transferred **72 hours after the lesson is completed** (dispute window).
- Parle keeps 20%. Stripe fees come out of Parle's share in Phase 1 (simple message to tutors: "you get 80%").
- Tutor Earnings page: upcoming, pending (in dispute window), paid, per-lesson breakdown.

## 6. Cancellations and problems
| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Learner cancels >= 24h before | Full refund to learner |
| Learner cancels < 24h before | No refund; tutor gets their 80% |
| Learner no-show (marked by tutor) | No refund; tutor gets 80%; learner can dispute within 72h |
| Tutor cancels any time | Full refund; cancellation counted on tutor record; 3 in 30 days -> admin review |
| Tutor no-show (reported by learner within 72h) | Payout held; admin reviews; refund if confirmed |
| Reschedule | Allowed once per booking up to 24h before, by either side with the other's acceptance |

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Tutor availability in one time zone shows correctly in another (test e.g. Toronto tutor, Tehran learner).
- [ ] Slots respect notice, buffer and existing bookings; the same slot cannot be booked twice.
- [ ] Stripe test-mode payment confirms a booking; both parties get emails with .ics and meeting link.
- [ ] Lesson brief is available to the tutor 24h before with all listed fields.
- [ ] Lesson notes update next week's plan and appear in the learner's history.
- [ ] Payout of 80% is created 72h after completion; the dispute flow holds it.
- [ ] Every cancellation rule in the table produces the right refund and payout in test mode.

## Out of scope (Phase 1)
Built-in video (Phase 4), lesson packages and discounts, recurring weekly bookings, tips, tutor-set currencies,
calendar sync (Google/Outlook).
