# Module 2 - Onboarding + Placement Test

Status: APPROVED 2026-10-08 (decision #20); BUILT, accepted for demo 2026-10-08 · Phase 1 · Build order 2 of 8

## Purpose
Feel like a consultation, not a form. Capture the learner's goal, constraints and real level so the
roadmap engine (Module 3) has everything it needs.

## Onboarding flow (~3 minutes, before account creation)
| Step | Question | Input | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why are you learning French? | Multi-select: Canada immigration, Canadian citizenship, Conversation, General fluency, Work, Job interview, School, Travel, Other (text) | Then "Which matters most?" picks the main goal |
| 2 | What do you want to reach? | Pre-filled per track (see below); General: choose A2 / B1 / B2 / C1 with plain-language descriptions | Learner can change the pre-filled target |
| 3 | When do you need it? | Date picker, or "No deadline" | Immigration only: "Have you booked your TEF/TCF test?" yes (date) / no |
| 4 | How would you describe your French today? | Never studied / A few words / Basic conversations / Comfortable but make mistakes / Fluent with gaps | Maps to starting level A0 / A1 / A2 / B1 / B2 for the adaptive test |
| 5 | How much time can you give? | Days of the week (chips) + minutes per day (10 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90) | Also "When could you take a lesson with a tutor?" time ranges (optional) |
| 6 | About you | Native language, time zone (auto), tutor budget per hour (optional: none / under $20 / $20-35 / $35-50 / $50+) | Budget "none" means the roadmap never suggests paid lessons |
| 7 | Create your account | Email + password or Google | Answers so far are kept and attached to the new account |

Track pre-fills:
- Canada immigration -> NCLC 7 in reading, writing, listening, speaking.
- Canadian citizenship -> NCLC 4 in listening and speaking.
- Conversation / General / other goals -> learner picks a CEFR target (default B1).

After step 7: "Let's find your real level" -> placement test (can be skipped and taken later; the roadmap is then built
from the self-assessment and marked "Unverified level").

## Placement test (15-20 minutes, adaptive)
| Section | Format | Items | Scoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Audio clip (AI voice) + multiple choice | 4-6 | Auto |
| Reading | Short text + multiple choice | 4-6 | Auto |
| Grammar & vocabulary | Multiple choice / fill the gap | 6-8 | Auto |
| Writing | One prompt at the estimated level (40-120 words) | 1 | AI rubric: task completion, grammar, vocabulary, coherence |
| Speaking | Two recorded answers, 30-60s each | 2 | AI on transcript + audio: task completion, grammar, vocabulary, fluency; pronunciation (beta) |

Adaptive rule: each auto-scored section starts at the self-assessed level; 2 correct in a row moves one level up,
2 wrong in a row moves one level down; the section ends after its item count or when the level is stable
(the same level answered at >= 70% for 3 items).

Speaking: requires microphone permission. If denied or skipped, speaking is "Not yet assessed" and the roadmap
schedules an early speaking check with the AI tutor.

Citizenship track: reading and writing sections are optional (shown as "Optional for your goal").

## Results screen
- Level per skill (CEFR) and, for Canada tracks, estimated NCLC per skill.
- Label: "Estimate - not an official score."
- Gap to target in plain language: "You're about 2 steps from NCLC 7 in speaking."
- CTA: "See my roadmap".
- The learner can retake the placement test once every 14 days.

## Question bank
- Items are generated by AI, then reviewed and approved by an admin or approved tutor before use (Module 8).
- Each item stores: skill, CEFR level, format, content, audio (for listening), correct answer, explanation, status.
- Launch minimum: 30 approved items per auto-scored skill per level A1-B2 (360 items), plus 5 writing and
  5 speaking prompts per level.
- Items are never generated live during a test.
- Audio for listening items is generated once (AI voice) and stored.

## Data produced
learner_profile (goals, main goal, target per skill, target date, test booked date, availability, tutor time
windows, budget, native language) and placement_result (level per skill, scores, per-item responses, date).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] All 7 onboarding steps work on mobile and desktop; back navigation keeps answers.
- [ ] Answers given before sign-up are attached to the account after sign-up (including Google sign-up).
- [ ] Track pre-fills match the table above and can be edited.
- [ ] Placement adapts up and down per the rule; a strong learner finishes higher than a weak one in testing.
- [ ] Mic denied -> test continues, speaking shows "Not yet assessed".
- [ ] Writing and speaking get a level and short feedback within 20 seconds of submission.
- [ ] Results screen shows per-skill levels, NCLC estimates for Canada tracks, and the estimate label.
- [ ] Skipping placement still produces a roadmap marked "Unverified level".

## Out of scope (Phase 1)
Official-exam-format sections (Phase 3), proctoring, retaking individual sections.
