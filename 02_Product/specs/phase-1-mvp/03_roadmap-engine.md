# Module 3 - Roadmap Engine + Today's Plan

Status: APPROVED 2026-10-08 (decision #20); amended 2026-10-10 (sections 4, 5, 9; decisions #27, #29 provisional) · Phase 1 · Build order 3 of 8

## Purpose
Turn goal + current level + target + date + available time into a roadmap, a weekly plan and a daily
"Today's Plan", and keep re-planning as evidence comes in. **This is the heart of the product.**

Principle: the engine is deterministic rules (testable, explainable). AI only writes the content of tasks.

## 1. Internal level scale
| CEFR | A0 | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 | C1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Score | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

Skills move in steps of 0.25. NCLC targets are converted with this approximation (to be validated with tutors
before launch):

| NCLC | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Score | 1.5 | 2.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 | 4.5 | 5.0 |

## 2. Roadmap (milestones)
- Skills: listening, reading, writing, speaking (grammar and vocabulary are tracked as sub-areas that feed all four).
- For each weighted skill: milestones every 0.5 between current and target (e.g. speaking 2.0 -> 2.5 -> 3.0 -> 3.5).
- Each milestone has a short "can-do" statement (e.g. "Describe your work and daily routine in the past tense").
- The roadmap screen shows milestones per skill on a timeline to the target date, plus a feasibility badge.

## 3. Skill weights per track
| Track | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canada Immigration | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Canadian Citizenship | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| General / Conversation / other | 1.2 | 0.8 | 0.6 | 1.4 |

## 4. Weekly plan
Inputs: weekly minutes M (sum of available days x minutes/day), skill gaps g_s = target_s - current_s.

1. Reserve the weekly check-in: 10 minutes on the last available day of the week.
2. If budget is not "none" and M >= 90: suggest one 45-minute tutor lesson per week, placed in the learner's
   tutor time window (shown as "Suggested", never auto-booked).
3. Remaining minutes are shared across skills:
   - raw weight w_s = track_weight_s x max(g_s, 0.25)  (0.25 keeps a reached skill in light maintenance)
   - share_s = w_s / sum(w), with a floor of 10% for every skill whose track weight > 0
   - weakness boost: the skill with the lowest recent AI mastery or the most "struggling" tutor marks gets
     +10 percentage points, taken proportionally from the others
4. Week 1 only: if speaking is "Not yet assessed" and weighted, schedule a 10-minute AI speaking check first.
5. First (partial) week, added 2026-10-10 (decision #29, provisional): a learner who joins mid-week gets a plan for
   the remaining available days of that week only, using only the remaining minutes. No tutor suggestion in that
   partial week unless at least 90 minutes and a day in the learner's tutor window remain. If no available day
   remains, the first plan starts the following week and Today says when ("Your plan starts <weekday, date>").
   The header shows the minutes actually scheduled, not the recurring weekly total.

## 5. Daily tasks (Today's Plan)
| Task type | Length | Delivered by |
|---|---|---|
| AI lesson (explain -> practice -> correct -> mini test) | 15-20 min | Module 4 |
| Vocabulary drill (track-themed word sets, spaced review) | 10 min | Engine + AI content |
| Listening exercise (AI-voiced clip + questions) | 10-15 min | Engine + AI content |
| Speaking practice with AI (scenario) | 10-15 min | Module 4 |
| Writing with AI correction | 15-20 min | Module 4 |
| Tutor lesson (booked) | 30-60 min | Module 6 |
| Weekly check-in | 10 min | Engine |

Content source for practice tasks (vocabulary sets, listening clips, speaking/writing prompts): generated by AI when
the week plan is built, at the learner's level and track topic, and stored with the task (listening audio via AI voice).
Unlike assessment items (Module 2), practice content is not human-reviewed before use; every task has a "Report a
problem" link that feeds an admin queue (Module 8). If generation fails, the task falls back to a stored item from
the approved bank at the same skill and level.

Rules:
- Fill each available day up to its minutes; at most 3 skills per day; never the same task type twice in a day.
  Added 2026-10-10: "filled" means 80-120% of the day's minutes; task lengths may flex inside the ranges above.
- Every task can be completed from Today: vocabulary and listening are answered with feedback; other tasks have
  "Mark as done" and "Skip". Completion drives minutes studied and the streak (section 8).
- Topics follow the track: immigration (work, housing, services, opinions), citizenship (everyday life, community,
  appointments, personal history), general (learner's chosen goal label).
- Each task shows: skill, minutes, why it's here ("Speaking is your biggest gap").

## 6. Re-planning
| Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|
| Weekly check-in completed | Update skill estimates (max +-0.25 per skill per week), rebuild next week |
| Tutor lesson notes submitted | Apply weakness boost from "struggling" marks, add homework as a task |
| AI mastery drops below 60% on a topic | Add a review AI lesson within 3 days |
| Missed tasks | Roll over to the next available day once; after that drop them and rebalance. A day is never filled above 120% of its minutes |
| Learner changes date, minutes or days | Rebuild from today |
| Placement retake | Replace skill estimates with new results, rebuild |

Plan differences (Module 7): **Free** re-plans on check-ins, missed tasks, settings changes and placement retakes.
**Premium** also re-plans during the week on tutor notes and AI mastery drops, and gets the weakness boost
(section 4, step 3). The feasibility check (section 7) is shown to everyone.

Every re-plan shows a one-line "What changed" note on Today's Plan (e.g. "More speaking this week: your check-in
showed listening is on track").

## 7. Feasibility check
Guided hours per step (approximation, to calibrate with real data):

| Step | A0->A1 | A1->A2 | A2->B1 | B1->B2 | B2->C1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hours | 90 | 100 | 180 | 200 | 250 |

- Required hours = hours to lift the largest weighted gap to target (pro-rated for 0.25 steps).
- Available hours = weekly minutes / 60 x weeks until target date. Each booked tutor hour counts 1.5x.
- Status: On track (available >= required) · Tight (>= 80%) · Unlikely (< 80%).
- Unlikely shows two options: minutes/day needed for the date, or the realistic date at current minutes.
- No deadline: show the projected date instead.

## 8. Progress
- Per skill: progress % = (current - start) / (target - start), capped 0-100.
- Overall goal readiness = weighted average using the track weights.
- Progress screen: skill levels over time, check-in history, minutes studied per week, streak (days with at least
  one completed task). No XP or leagues in Phase 1.

## 9. Weekly check-in
10 minutes from the approved bank: 3 listening, 3 reading or grammar, 1 short speaking answer and 1 short writing answer
when those skills are weighted. Scores at, above or below the current estimate move the estimate by +0.25, 0 or -0.25.

Scoring rule, added 2026-10-10 (decision #27, provisional):
- Each set of three questions: 0-1 correct = below (-0.25), 2 = at (0), 3 = above (+0.25).
- Grammar replaces reading when reading is not weighted; it is stored as sub-area evidence and moves no skill estimate.
- Writing is scored by the AI writing scorer (same as placement) and compared with the current estimate. If scoring
  fails: "Not scored this week", no change.
- Speaking never moves the estimate while speech cannot be assessed: the learner's self-rating is stored and shown as
  "Self-rated - not assessed". Revisit when speech-to-text exists (after export).
- Options are shuffled per display; answers are tracked by option id. The "What changed" note names only skills
  whose estimate moved from scored evidence.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A new learner gets a roadmap, weekly plan and Today's Plan right after placement (or after skipping it).
- [ ] Citizenship learners get no reading/writing tasks unless they opt in.
- [ ] Day minutes are never exceeded by more than 20%; no task type repeats in a day.
- [ ] A check-in result changes next week's allocation and shows a "What changed" note.
- [ ] Unrealistic goals show "Unlikely" with both options computed.
- [ ] Budget "none" never produces a tutor suggestion.
- [ ] Unit tests cover allocation, floors, boost, rollover and feasibility with fixed inputs.

## Out of scope (Phase 1)
AI coach messages (Phase 2), mock-exam driven re-plans (Phase 3), calendar sync.
