# Parle idea bank beyond phases 1-5 (brainstorm of 2026-10-12)

Status: IDEAS, not approved specs. These are additions to what phases 2 to 5 already describe
(`phase-2-engagement.md` ... `phase-5-ecosystem.md`). Agent's list; the founder picked six to prioritise
(decision #32).

## Prioritised (founder + agent, 2026-10-12)
| # | Idea | Why | Earliest place it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Outcome guarantee on the exam track**: reach the target level by the test date if the plan is followed, or get extra months free | Strongest reason to choose Parle over a generic app. The Module 3 feasibility maths already knows who qualifies ("Likely" plans only) | Policy + eligibility rule after export; needs Phase 3 readiness estimates to be credible |
| 4 | **Lesson recordings turned into practice**: with consent, a tutor lesson becomes a transcript, a mistake list and next week's exercises | Where the tutor marketplace and the AI tutor reinforce each other | After export (needs built-in video, Phase 4 item 4.2). Phase 1 already has the manual version: tutor lesson notes feed the roadmap (Module 6) |
| 11 | **Free public level check** with a shareable result | Top of the funnel; gives social content (Content Carousel) somewhere to send people | The Module 2 placement test made public without sign-up; after export |
| 8 | **Lesson packs and a tutor-plus-AI bundle** | Smooths tutor income, raises spend per learner | Module 6 / 7 territory; Stripe complexity argues for after export |
| 13 | **Dispute and no-show handling with evidence** (join times, messages) | Refunds without judgement calls | Module 6 (booking, cancellation table) + Module 8 (admin) |
| 12 | **Tutor supply planning**: forecast demand by time zone and specialty | Recruit before learners cannot find a slot | Module 8 admin metrics; `05_Operations/tutors/recruiting/` |

## Not prioritised yet
2. Tutor quality loop: rank tutors by their learners' measured progress, not only stars.
3. Progress certificate: shareable, verifiable record of hours, level history and mock results ("not an official score").
5. Real-life missions: tasks outside the app (book an appointment in French, leave a voicemail), reported to the tutor.
6. Pronunciation coaching at the sound level per native language (Persian, Arabic, Spanish, Hindi).
7. Speaking partners: two learners at the same level, AI as moderator.
9. Family and couple plans.
10. Referral credit both ways (learners, and tutors who bring their own students).
14. Public trust page: how levels are estimated, how tutors are vetted, what the AI does and does not do.
